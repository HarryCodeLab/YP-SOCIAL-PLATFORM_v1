1. The YP ANTHEM

The YP anthem can be sung during parade. This is possible in YP section where they are few groups for the rally.

If the YP anthem is sung during parade, the patrol is called to dressing so as to maintain their arm spacing and position.
The song is intoned;immediately the YP members band too the ground, place their staff across, right to the left in front of them (horizontally) with the middle of the staff in between the legs.
They start singing immediately they standup. After singing this anthem, the GL calls "staff" and the YP members pick up their staff and assume rest position and wait for further commands.

2.The YP PROMISE

The YP promise is said during meetings, rallies and many other YP events as a reminder to the YPs to not forget why they became YPs in the first place

. The YP promise stands as a commitment to know Christ and do everything with Christ including in our deeds, thoughts and words.
The YP promise remains to stand as a fountain for responsible living which should be adopted by Young Presbyterians.

The YP AIM

Aims of YP work are based on Matthew 18:5; 10; 14

1.To enable the Young Presbyterians become dedicated to Christ
2.To develop the character of the young people so that one day they might grow up to full mature men and women.
3.To develop a sense of fellowship among youth by enabling them to play and pray together.
4.To develop in youth the sense of responsibility towards meeting the social needs of the families, neighbors and other citizens.
5.To develop their conscience and a personal commitment to the Christian faith, in church and youth movement.
